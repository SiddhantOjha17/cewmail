Purpose: A polite nudge on a previous email, proposal, or conversation that hasn't received a response yet.

Tone: Friendly, brief, and low-pressure -- never guilt-trippy or pushy.

Structure to follow:
1. Reference the earlier email/conversation briefly (what it was about, and roughly when, if given in the context).
2. Restate in one line why it matters or what the ask was, so the recipient doesn't have to dig up the old thread.
3. Ask directly but politely for an update or next step.
4. Offer an easy out (e.g. "no worries if now isn't a good time, just let me know") if appropriate to the context.
5. Sign off warmly.

Keep it short -- 3-5 sentences. The goal is to prompt a reply, not to repeat the entire original message.
