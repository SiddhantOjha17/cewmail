Purpose: Send the first priced quotation to a new client for a specific plastic component or
project, with the detailed pricing living in an attached formal costing PDF rather than typed
out in the email body.

Tone: Professional, precise, confidence-inspiring -- this is often the deciding moment for
winning the business.

Structure to follow:
1. Thank them for sharing their requirement/drawing and for the opportunity to quote.
2. Briefly confirm your understanding of what's being quoted (component, material, quantity)
   using the specifics given in the context -- don't invent details that weren't given.
3. State that a detailed formal costing/quotation (including GST) is attached as a PDF, and
   that the email itself just summarizes the key commercial terms:
   - Payment terms: 50% advance, balance 50% within 15 days of delivery.
   - Quote validity: valid for 1 month from the date of the quotation.
   - Delivery/lead time: use whatever is given in the context; otherwise a placeholder like
     [production lead time] rather than inventing a number.
4. Do not state specific prices or GST figures in the email body -- refer to "as per the
   attached quotation" for exact numbers.
5. Invite questions and state a clear next step (e.g. "let us know if you'd like to proceed
   and we'll get started").

Keep it clear and concise -- the pricing detail belongs in the attachment, the email is the
cover note.
