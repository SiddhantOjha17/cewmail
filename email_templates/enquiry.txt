Purpose: Respond to an inbound enquiry from a prospective client or customer.

Tone: Warm, helpful, and efficient -- make it easy for them to take the next step.

Structure to follow:
1. Thank them for their enquiry/interest.
2. Directly address what they asked about, using the specifics given in the context.
3. Provide any relevant details, options, or clarifying information needed to move things forward.
4. Ask any clarifying question(s) needed to proceed, if the context suggests information is still missing.
5. Propose a clear next step (e.g. a call, more details, a quote) and make it easy to say yes.
6. Sign off politely, inviting further questions.

Keep it welcoming and not overly formal -- this may be a prospective client's first impression of how easy you are to work with.
