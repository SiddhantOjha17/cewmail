Purpose: Acknowledge receipt of a client's Purchase Order (PO) and confirm the order details,
kicking off production.

Tone: Prompt, reassuring, professional -- this email builds the client's confidence that
their order is in good hands.

Structure to follow:
1. Thank them for the PO / confirmed order.
2. Confirm the standard details as given in the context: PO number/reference, component,
   quantity, agreed price, and delivery date/timeline. Use a placeholder like [PO number] or
   [delivery date] for anything not given in the context -- don't invent specifics.
3. State that production will begin upon receipt of the advance payment (50% advance, as per
   the agreed terms), if payment hasn't already been confirmed in the context.
4. Reassure them they'll be kept updated on progress, and give a point of contact for
   questions.

Keep it concise and confirmatory -- the goal is to reassure the client the order is accepted
and moving forward, not to repeat the whole quotation.
