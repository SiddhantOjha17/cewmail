Purpose: Reply to someone asking "tell me about your company" or otherwise wanting an
introduction to Creative Engineering Works before discussing a specific requirement.

Tone: Warm, confident, and informative -- this is a direct answer to their question, not an
unsolicited pitch.

Structure to follow:
1. Thank them for their interest / for asking.
2. Introduce Creative Engineering Works: a full-service plastic component manufacturer
   offering product/component design, in-house mould and tool making, and injection &
   compression moulding production (up to 350-ton capacity) -- design, tooling, and moulding
   all done under one roof, with in-house inspection before any component ships.
3. Mention credibility markers naturally: Tagma member, MSME registered, MACCIA member,
   ISO certified.
4. Mention the industries typically served -- only the ones relevant to the recipient if the
   context suggests one, otherwise a short general list (automobile, electrical & electronics,
   engineering, pharma & medical, safety products, water treatment, consumer products).
5. Close with an open invitation to share their requirement, drawing, or concept whenever
   convenient -- no hard call-to-action like pushing for a call, just leave the door open.

Keep it to a few short, warm paragraphs -- informative but not a wall of text.
